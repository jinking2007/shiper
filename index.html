Who we are

Giving a voice to the voiceless

Since 2016, PawGuardians has been on the front lines of animal welfare. We operate a network of foster homes, veterinary clinics, and adoption centers across the country.

Our team of 200+ volunteers works tirelessly to rescue injured, abandoned, and neglected animals — giving them a second chance at a happy life.
